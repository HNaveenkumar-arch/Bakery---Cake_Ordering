Classic Chocolate Cake

Rich, soft and deeply chocolatey.

Prep: 20 Mins Bake: 35 Mins

Key Ingredients

  • 2 cups All-purpose flour
  • ¾ cup Unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 ½ tsp Baking powder & baking soda
  • 1 cup Milk & ½ cup vegetable oil

Baker's Tip

Use hot water or hot brewed coffee at the end of the batter mixing to enhance the rich chocolate flavor.

02

Vanilla Bean Cupcakes

Light, fluffy and delicately sweet.

Expand
Prep: 15 Mins Bake: 20 Mins

Key Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups Cake flour
  • 1 cup Granulated sugar
  • ½ cup Unsalted butter (room temp)
  • 2 tsp Pure vanilla bean paste

Baker's Tip

Do not overmix the batter. Mix just until the dry ingredients are incorporated to keep the cupcakes fluffy.

Homemade Choco Cookies

Crispy edges with soft, gooey centres.

Expand
Prep: 15 Mins Bake: 12 Mins

Key Ingredients

  • 2 ¼ cups All-purpose flour
  • ¾ cup Brown sugar & ¾ cup white sugar
  • 1 cup Softened butter
  • 2 cups Semi-sweet chocolate chips

Baker's Tip

Chill the cookie dough in the fridge for at least 30 minutes before baking to prevent them from spreading too flat.

Baking Secrets

Little Tips.
Big Baking Magic.

Discover simple baking tips and tricks to make every sweet moment completely delicious.

01

How To Keep Cakes Moist

  • Storage: Wrap the cake tightly in plastic wrap while still warm.
  • Frosting: Frosting acts as a sealant. Cover it quickly.
  • Freshness: Store sliced cakes in airtight containers.
02

Choosing The Right Size

  • Small Parties: A 6-inch cake is perfect for 8-10 people.
  • Birthdays: Standard 8-inch cakes serve 14-16 guests comfortably.
  • Large Events: Opt for multi-tier or large sheet cakes.
03

Cake Decoration Ideas

  • Minimalist: Smooth buttercream with a touch of gold leaf.
  • Floral Looks: Use food-safe flowers for a romantic finish.
  • Choco Drip: Add chocolate edges and curls for drama.
